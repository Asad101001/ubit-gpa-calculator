UBIT BSCS Batch 2024–28 Academic Portal

This is the official community-built academic portal for students of BSCS Batch 2024–28 at Umaer Basha Institute of Information Technology (UBIT), Department of Computer Science (DCS), University of Karachi, Pakistan.

Features

  • GPA Calculator for Semester 1, 2, and 3 using UBIT's 4.0 grading scale
  • Cumulative CGPA calculation across all completed semesters
  • Live class results portal with marks for all batch students
  • Target CGPA advisor that tells you the GPA needed in remaining semesters
  • Subject-wise performance analytics and charts
  • Anonymous class leaderboard
  • Privacy controls — hide your results from public view
